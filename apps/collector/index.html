1Payment link

ZARINPAL_MERCHANT_ID is not set. Copy .env.example to .env, fill it in and restart the server.

2Gateway page URL

Open the link, and once you reach the page with the card form and captcha, copy its URL from the address bar and paste it here.

3Page HTML

Once per gateway session. Best: DevTools (⌥⌘I), right-click the <html> element, Copy → Copy outerHTML, so JS-rendered parts are included. Or view-source (⌥⌘U), or drop / pick a saved .html file.

4Captchas

On the gateway, right-click the captcha, choose Copy Image, then press ⌘V anywhere on this page.
Then refresh the captcha on the gateway and repeat; every refresh is a new sample.
